# Portfolio design direction

## Intent

Present Wannyam Terkuma Benedict as a practical software developer, digital skills trainer, and data collection specialist for remote teams. Keep the site calm, direct, and easy to scan.

## Visual system

- Use a near-black default theme with off-white type, muted gray supporting text, fine borders, and rounded pill navigation.
- Keep the current portrait, project screenshots, names, claims, and links as the source of truth. Reference portfolios guide layout and interaction only; do not reuse another person's identity, copy, or images.
- Use Manrope for strong headings, DM Sans for body text, and DM Mono for compact labels.
- Keep pages responsive from mobile through desktop. The narrow-screen menu stays keyboard accessible and closes on Escape, outside click, or navigation.

## Motion and interaction

- Type the rotating role line, reveal sections on scroll, stagger project and experience rows, and count the hero metrics once when they enter view.
- Keep animations short and optional. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; all content remains readable without JavaScript.
- Provide a light-theme toggle and persist the visitor's choice when browser storage is available.
- Use visible focus styles and labeled controls. Do not introduce a chatbot or send form data to a service unless a real need and endpoint are confirmed.

## Content rules

- Feature verified work: SecureVault AutoBackup, VoteSecure, the Flight Transport booking platform, Ngosue International School, the Field Data Quality Workbench, and CrownTech Academy training.
- Clearly label concept artwork as illustration. Do not invent client results, source links, technologies, or remote-work credentials.
- Keep the printable résumé as a separate page linked from navigation and hero actions.
